Practice project

Store Manager

A simple Angular e-commerce app for beauty and skin-care products. Products come from the DummyJSON API; users can browse, view details, add new products (saved in localStorage) and delete the ones they added. Includes routing, @Input / @Output communication, an HttpClient service, a custom pipe, and loading and error handling.
